Overview

Craft, taste, and culture at global scale.

I joined Apple as the company's first-ever Social Strategy Intern, embedded in the Worldwide Marketing Communications team. My summer was spent deep in short-form vertical content and social strategy for some of Apple's biggest moments, including the iPhone 18 Pro Max launch, the #ShotOniPhone franchise, and campaigns across Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube.

How does a brand everyone already knows show up on social in a way that feels new, while keeping human craft, taste, and creativity first?

I researched and evaluated new creative approaches across Apple's social channels and explored how to reach younger audiences in surprising, platform-native ways. I sat between strategy, design, and performance: reading culture, briefing creative, giving feedback on edits, and analyzing how the work landed. That work led to a workshop for ~600 creatives, strategists, and brand managers across Worldwide Marketing.

Gen Z audience insights → a reusable creative framework

Audited 2,000+ social videos, competitor campaigns, and emerging platform trends to find Gen Z behavioral patterns. I turned the findings into a reusable creative framework and led workshops for ~600 directors, managers, and cross-functional partners.

Strategy lead for 20+ designers

Translated early-stage concepts into platform-ready executions for a cohort of 20+ designers. I connected design thinking with audience behavior, format constraints, and distribution strategy.

#ShotOniPhone content strategy & creative direction

Developed content strategy and creative direction for the #ShotOniPhone franchise on @apple, and competitive inspiration research for @helloapple. I defined visual and narrative territories across Instagram and TikTok that informed campaign ideation, content planning, and go-to-market positioning.

Signature project

My biggest project was a research-to-strategy pipeline. I studied what makes short-form content work for Gen Z, then packaged it so designers, strategists, and brand managers across Worldwide Marketing could apply it to their own briefs. As Apple's first ever intern in the Worldwide strategy team, it felt particularly exciting to demonstrate from within the company how many Gen Z tendencies can expand what we know and think of in terms of social media and product marketing.

Reflections

What I learned.

Being the first person in a role means nobody hands you a playbook. You build it. I learned to move between a 2,000-video audit, a designer's rough cut, and a room of 600 people, and to make the same idea useful at each level.

I also learned how much restraint matters. At Apple, the most strategic call is often what not to post, which caption to cut, or which clip shows the craft best. Data told me what was working. Taste decided what was worth making. I want to keep working where brand, culture, creative, and technology meet.
